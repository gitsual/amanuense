Confluence docs always lag one step behind the code.

So I built **amanuense**: it reads code changes and team requests, then proposes which pages to update and how.

→ Shows every change line by line, against what Confluence says today.
→ Flags any data the AI can't back up with a source.
→ Keeps a log of who changed what, and why.
→ Turns the exported space into an Obsidian vault with one command.

You approve. You paste. Nothing reaches Confluence until a person reviews it.

Runs locally on Linux and Windows. Works with Claude, Codex, Gemini, Copilot or Ollama, or with no AI at all: paste the task into a chat.

Open source, MIT licensed: github.com/gitsual/amanuense
